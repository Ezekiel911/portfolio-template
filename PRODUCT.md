# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, Tailwind utility classes, and vanilla JavaScript.

## Users

Template users: developers and students who want a responsive, accessible, repository-ready portfolio.

## Product Purpose

Provide a no-data portfolio foundation that can be customized with verified personal facts.

## Positioning

The template separates identity, projects, education, and professional links so private or unverified information is not required.

## Operating Context

The site is a public, mobile-first portfolio that must remain easy to scan on a resume or interview visit and must work on GitHub Pages without a backend.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static navigation, project filtering, project dialogs, theme switching, and project screenshots.
- Contact details and a browser-generated email action.
- No backend, database, analytics, or form-processing service is currently required.
- Only verified portfolio content and project information may be presented as factual.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: `Your Name`.
- Approved project screenshots.
- Professional, approachable, security-minded tone.

## Evidence on Hand

- No personal profile facts included.
- Privacy-safe initials monogram instead of a personal portrait.
- Neutral screenshot placeholder under `images/template-placeholder.svg`.
- No direct email address or personal social profile in the public site.
- Optional public profile links only.

## Product Principles

- Lead with truthful, verifiable claims.
- Make projects understandable without inflated outcomes.
- Keep contact and navigation actions obvious and accessible.
- Preserve responsive behavior and a usable no-JavaScript baseline.
